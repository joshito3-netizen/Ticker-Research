# Decision Memo — Microsoft Corporation

**To:** Buy-side investment committee

**From:** AI task-force analyst

**Decision date / evidence cutoff:** September 9, 2026

**Security:** Microsoft (MSFT) common stock; fund currently holds no position

**Decision horizon:** 12 months; valuation uses long-duration cash flows

## Decision and committee ask

**Watch/defer; do not initiate at the $491.65 September 9 close.** The pro-forma-driven FCFF
DCF supports a causal range of **$112–$356 per diluted share**, with a **$230 base case**. The
market price exceeds even the modeled upside case. Salesforce supplies conflicting reference
evidence: its FY2026 GAAP P/E implies **$562**, while its EV/Revenue implies **$251**. The methods
are not averaged because they encode different economics and Salesforce does not represent
Azure infrastructure or Microsoft's consumer mix.

Place MSFT on the watch list. Reopen the initiation decision if price enters the supported range,
or if new evidence raises the base valuation while preserving at least a 20% committee margin of
safety. Do not treat the $356 upside boundary as an automatic purchase price.

## Evidence and as-of boundary

Microsoft reported FY2026 revenue of **$331.839 billion**, operating income of **$155.237
billion**, and Intelligent Cloud revenue of **$137.791 billion**. FY2026 cash additions to PP&E
were **$115.948 billion**, new finance-lease assets were **$24.608 billion**, and finance-lease
liabilities ended at **$66.594 billion**. These lease facts matter because a cash-capex-only DCF
would omit a material investment channel. Filing facts are from Microsoft's Form 10-K filed July
29, 2026 and cross-checked to the same-day investor-relations release.

Reported net income of **$133.749 billion** includes a **$4.963 billion after-tax OpenAI
investment gain**. The normalized base removes that gain, leaving **$128.786 billion** and
normalized diluted EPS of **$17.28**. Forecast other income includes cash yield and financing
cost, but no recurring investment gain.

The base DCF discounts FY2027–FY2031 economic FCFF at **9.5% WACC**, uses **3.0% perpetual
growth**, subtracts cash and finance-lease investment, and bridges enterprise value through excess
cash, 50% recovery of equity-investment carrying value, term debt, and finance-lease liabilities.
Terminal value is **81.0%** of enterprise value, making terminal and reinvestment assumptions
material.

## Material assumptions and sensitivities

The base forecast starts FY2027 growth at 12% for Productivity, 25% for Intelligent Cloud, and 2%
for More Personal Computing, then fades each path. EBITDA margin is 58%; cash capex falls from 29%
to 21% of revenue and finance-lease additions fall from 7.5% to 5.5%. These are analyst estimates,
not guidance.

One-at-a-time tests identify cash-capex intensity as the largest value driver across the selected
ranges (**$41/share span**), followed by cloud growth (**$27**), finance-lease additions (**$21**),
EBITDA margin (**$17**), and Productivity growth (**$14**). The WACC/terminal-growth grid spans
**$170–$336**. Raising cash capex one percentage point in every forecast year reduces base value
by **$10.27/share**; raising cloud growth one point increases it by **$4.63/share**.

The market price requires an approximately **+20.6 percentage-point uniform shift** to all three
five-year segment-growth paths while margins, reinvestment, WACC, terminal growth, bridge
balances, shares, and horizon remain fixed. That is one price-consistent assumption set, not proof
of overvaluation.

## Risks, failure conditions, and monitoring triggers

- **AI infrastructure returns:** monitor Intelligent Cloud growth against cash capex plus new
  finance-lease assets. Escalate if combined investment stays above 30% of revenue while cloud
  growth drops below 20%.
- **Margin conversion:** monitor EBIT margin after depreciation and finance-lease interest.
  Reconsider the forecast if margin falls below 45% without an offsetting acceleration in durable
  cash growth.
- **Backlog conversion:** monitor commercial RPO conversion into reported revenue and operating
  cash, not RPO growth alone.
- **Hidden duration:** monitor finance-lease liabilities and not-yet-commenced datacenter
  commitments. New obligations unsupported by demand are a do-not-initiate trigger.
- **Model risk:** terminal value, aggregate depreciation, fixed diluted shares, and a single
  qualified peer limit precision. Accounting agreement does not validate the forecast.

## Action requested and next review

Approve **watch/defer**, no position. Review at the next earnings release or earlier if MSFT
enters the supported range. Require a fresh filing update, capex/lease return test, and committee
margin-of-safety check before any initiation.
