## Dated correction addendum — October 19, 2026

Edition A called June 30, 2026 the valuation date. June 30 is the FY2026 financial-statement date,
but the Form 10-K was not filed until July 29; using its information as of June 30 would create a
look-ahead problem. Edition B therefore distinguishes:

- financial-statement date: June 30, 2026;
- filing availability date: July 29, 2026; and
- final valuation/comparison date and evidence cutoff: September 9, 2026, consistent with the
  saved Week 3 and Week 4 market work.

### What changed from Edition A to Edition B, and why?

The recommendation did not change, but it is now decision-ready. Edition A's concern about AI
infrastructure returns became an articulated financial mechanism: cash PP&E and finance-lease
assets reduce economic FCFF, depreciation affects EBIT, lease interest affects earnings, and
lease liabilities reduce equity value. The final timing boundary removes look-ahead, the model
produces a range rather than a point claim, and initiation now has observable reconsideration and
