# Project 1 — Edition A: Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)

**Decision and user.** This is a first-pass research note for an investor deciding whether to initiate a position in Microsoft common stock.

**Company and valuation date.** Microsoft Corporation (CIK 0000789019); valuation date **June 30, 2026**, the balance-sheet date of the current annual filing. The primary source is Microsoft’s Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, filed July 29, 2026: [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/0001193125-26-323660-index.htm) and [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm). Accession number: **0001193125-26-323660**. Currency is **U.S. dollars**; financial-statement amounts below are **USD millions** unless stated otherwise.

## Initial thesis

**Watch / defer; do not initiate yet.** The filing shows an unusually strong cloud-led growth engine, but the investment case needs work on the durability and return on the AI-infrastructure spend before a valuation can be defended. The 2026 headline earnings figure also includes a material gain on OpenAI investments, so it is not a clean proxy for recurring earning power.

## Synthetic enterprise-to-equity bridge check

This is the class-style mechanical bridge, performed on a synthetic $100,000 million enterprise value. It uses operating debt at carrying amount and treats cash plus short-term investments as non-operating liquidity. No preferred equity or noncontrolling interest adjustment is included because none is identified in this first-pass bridge.

| Bridge item | USD millions |
|---|---:|
| Synthetic enterprise value | $100,000 |
| Add: cash and cash equivalents | $20,935 |
| Add: short-term investments | $55,908 |
| Less: current portion of long-term debt | ($9,227) |
| Less: long-term debt | ($31,067) |
| **Implied equity value** | **$136,549** |

**Check:** $100,000 + $20,935 + $55,908 - $9,227 - $31,067 = **$136,549 million**. On 7,453 million diluted shares, this synthetic equity value equals **$18.32 per diluted share**. This validates the enterprise-to-equity direction and arithmetic; it is not a Microsoft valuation or price target.

## Consequential assumptions

1. Azure and Microsoft Cloud growth can remain strong enough to earn acceptable returns on incremental AI data-center infrastructure.
2. Commercial customers will continue to pay for Copilot and Azure AI services, rather than AI usage becoming mostly a cost layer or being competed away.
3. Cash and short-term investments are available to equity holders for bridge purposes; the next version will test taxes, repatriation, and operating-liquidity needs.
4. The OpenAI investment gain is non-core for forecasting; the base earnings view should separate it from operating performance.

## Material unknowns

- The incremental capital expenditure, depreciation, and unit economics attributable to AI capacity and AI products.
- Azure’s growth, margin, and customer concentration disclosed at the level needed to underwrite a normalized forecast.
- The size, timing, and economics of commercial remaining performance obligation conversion into revenue and cash flow.
- A defensible valuation multiple or DCF discount rate, and the market price on the selected valuation date.
- Whether leases, unfunded commitments, investments, and other claims require adjustments to the simple bridge.

## Research plan

1. Build a three-statement FY27–FY31 model from the 10-K and subsequent 10-Qs, explicitly separating the OpenAI investment result from operating income.
2. Model Intelligent Cloud and Productivity & Business Processes separately; use Azure growth, commercial-seat growth, pricing, and cloud gross margin as key sensitivities.
3. Reconstruct AI capital intensity: property-and-equipment additions, depreciation, leases, contractual commitments, and implied returns on incremental invested capital.
4. Reconcile cash, debt, leases, investments, and share count into a fully specified EV-to-equity bridge.
5. Value using a DCF and cross-check against peer and Microsoft historical valuation ranges; initiate only if the resulting margin of safety remains after downside cases.
